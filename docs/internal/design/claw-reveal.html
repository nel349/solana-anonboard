Solana × Midnight · claw-reveal study

The paper is the surface everyone sees. The claw marks show what's underneath.

Membership lives in the dark on Midnight; the reveal is Solana's own gradient, torn open — not printed on top.

Three claw languages

How the reveal is built

  1. Stack, don't paintThe Solana gradient, logo and bloom are a full layer behind the paper — never drawn on top. The reveal is real depth, not a decal.
  2. Punch the paper with a maskAn SVG <mask> is white everywhere except the claw shapes, which are black — so the paper (and the wordmark printed on it) becomes a hole exactly there.
  3. Tear the edgesfeTurbulence + feDisplacementMap jitter the claw outline so the rip is ragged like torn paper, not a clean vector slit.
  4. Give it depthA drop-shadow on the masked paper casts a soft shadow into each gash, so the sheet reads as sitting above the glow.
  5. Let the light spillA blurred, screen-blended copy of the gashes leaks Solana color onto the surrounding paper — the "glowing from behind" tell.
Why it feels Solana without a logo slap. The brand shows up as light and color coming through a surface, the way the gradient reads on Solana's own material — earned, not stamped. The paper stays a neutral kraft so the gradient is the only saturated thing on screen.
